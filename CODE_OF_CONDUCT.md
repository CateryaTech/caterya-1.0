# Code of conduct

We want this to be a place where people with different backgrounds can do careful, honest technical work together.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; critique ideas and evidence, not people; assume good faith but verify claims; credit others' work; disclose conflicts of interest
when you evaluate or review something you have a stake in; welcome newcomers and non-native English speakers (Indonesian and English are both fine in issues).

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, or knowingly misrepresenting results or evidence.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants. Report concerns privately to **aryhharyanto@proton.me**. Reports are handled
confidentially and impartially; a maintainer with a conflict of interest recuses themselves.

This policy applies to all project spaces and to people representing the project in public.
